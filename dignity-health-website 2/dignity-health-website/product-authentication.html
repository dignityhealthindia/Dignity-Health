PRODUCT AUTHENTICATION

Grey-market and counterfeit supplements are common in India. Here's how to confirm your Dignity Health product is genuine.

How to verify your product

01

Check the batch code

Every jar carries a printed batch code on the base label — confirm this matches the format DH-YYMM-XXX.

02

Scan or enter online

Enter the batch code below or scan the QR code on the label to pull up that batch's lab test certificate.

03

Buy from verified sellers

What genuine Dignity Health packaging includes

  • FSSAI license number printed on every label
  • Tamper-evident inner seal beneath the lid
