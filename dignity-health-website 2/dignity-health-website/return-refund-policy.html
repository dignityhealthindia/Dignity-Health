Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Eligibility for returns

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for products that are unopened, unused, and in original sealed packaging. Due to the consumable nature of our products, opened jars cannot be returned unless defective.

Damaged or incorrect items

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the product and packaging
  • We'll arrange a free replacement or full refund for verified damaged, defective or incorrect items
  • No return shipping cost is charged to you in these cases

Refund process

Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of us receiving the returned item. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded via bank transfer or store credit.

Non-returnable situations

  • Products opened or used, unless defective
  • Products returned without prior authorization
  • Requests made after 7 days of delivery

How to initiate a return

Email enquiry@mydignity.in with your order number, reason for return, and photos if applicable. Our team will confirm eligibility and share pickup or drop-off instructions within 24–48 hours.
